# Preface

Wispr Flow is not a transcriber. It is a rewriter.

Hold a key, talk, and finished text appears where the cursor was. Not what was said. What was meant. The filler is gone, the punctuation lands in the right places, the half-sentence that doubled back on itself comes out clean. Call it the Rewrite Pass, because a language model sits between the microphone and the screen and quietly fixes the speaker on the way through. Everything Flow does well and everything it does badly starts there.

By mid-2026 the tool had a large paying base and a sprawling help center. What it did not have was a book. The documentation existed, but it existed as more than a hundred separate articles, each answering one question, none of them meant to be read end to end. Anyone who wanted to actually understand the tool had to click, read, click, read, and assemble the picture alone. That is a poor way to learn anything nontrivial.

This book is the assembly. One continuous read, the kind that survives on an e-reader on a couch, instead of a hundred open browser tabs. It is built for the human who is a week into Flow, newly on a paid plan, and after the whole shape of the thing in one sitting rather than a scavenger hunt through a support site.

A few things about how to read it.

It is independent and unofficial. Wispr Flow did not produce it, review it, or endorse it. The product names belong to their owners. What is here was synthesized from the official help center and from independent research run across several sources, then checked against itself.

It is a snapshot. The app ships changes at roughly a monthly cadence, which means some specific number, menu path, or limit in these pages will eventually be wrong. Where a claim is sensitive to versions, it carries a date. When a screen does not match the text, trust the screen, and let the date on the claim tell you how stale it might be.

It is honest about what it knows. Three kinds of claim live in this book, and each one is marked. Documented means the help center says so. Inferred means the sources point hard at it without stating it outright. Test-it means the answer was never nailed down anywhere and the only real authority is the device in your hand. The markers are not hedging. They are the line between a fact and a good guess, drawn in the open so the reader can tell which is which.

It does not have to be read in order. The foundations and the output chapters apply to everyone. The four platform chapters, Windows, macOS, iPhone, and Android, stand alone, so a Windows reader never has to wade through the iPhone keyboard and an Android reader can skip the Mac permission dance. The workflow chapters are self-contained. The chapter on teams and enterprise can be ignored entirely by anyone running Flow alone.

The rest of the book assumes one thing about its reader: full attention, and no need for cheerleading. Flow is fast when it is trusted and maddening when it is fought, and most of the friction people hit with it traces back to expecting a transcriber and getting a rewriter instead. Learn where the rewrite helps. Learn where it overreaches. The rest is detail.

Talk instead of type. The machine will handle the typing.
